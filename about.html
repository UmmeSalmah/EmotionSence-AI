💡 About EmotionSense AI

Emotion-aware intelligence for a more empathetic digital world 💖

🚀 Our Mission

EmotionSense AI is on a mission to humanize technology. We decode text and facial expressions using state-of-the-art AI models to help individuals, educators, and businesses understand emotions more deeply.

Whether it’s analyzing feedback, enabling better human-computer interaction, or supporting mental wellness — we aim to bring emotional intelligence into the digital age.

🧠 How It Works

  • 💬 Text Analysis: Sentiment & emotion classification using Google Gemini
  • 📸 Face Detection: Real-time emotion capture with OpenCV + CNN
  • 🎯 Multi-modal Fusion: Compare face & text moods for context
  • 📁 Personalized Feedback: Confidence scores & sentiment mapping
